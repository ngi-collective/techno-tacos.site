# Feedback round 2

Team answers from `transcript.md`, session of 2026-11-03. The questions are the eight from the previous round.

1. Sizes: everything grows together at 3 times life size. The B key still switches to real size. (accepted recommendation)
2. City: remove it and show open ocean, with stars and the moon at night. Keep the houses behind the dunes. (accepted recommendation)
3. Turtle movement: flippers row one side at a time, the body rocks, and they crawl in short bursts with rests, at about half the old speed. "The worst part was how it looked like they were sliding through the sand."
4. Nest: "No, keep the nest at the same distance, but give us a key to make the turtles speed up or slow down."
5. Setup: volunteers carry the box, walk the tunnel's end to the water, pour sand from a bucket, and swing a mallet at each stake. (accepted recommendation)
6. Predators: "Yes, the animations should be much better. Either generate or find higher quality models that we can use for free."
7. Tunnel floor: same sand texture as the beach, with soft bumps and turtle tracks. (accepted recommendation)
8. Anything else: "the sand also looked fake and had too little color variation."

## Follow-up answers

The team accepted every recommendation.

1. Speed key: `-` slows down and `+` speeds up, in steps of 1/4x, 1/2x, 1x, 2x, and 4x, with the current speed on screen. Turtles and predators change speed together.
2. Sliding: hatchlings push forward only when their flippers dig in and stop between strokes. They kick up small puffs of sand and leave flipper tracks that slowly fade.
3. Sand colors: dark wet sand with a shiny edge near the water, pale dry sand with uneven patches higher up, a darker strip of shells and seaweed along the high-tide line, and tufts of beach grass on the dunes.

## Model and sand choices

The team accepted every recommendation.

1. Turtle: the Sketchfab animated sea turtle, with the free no-account model as the backup.
2. Seagull: the Sketchfab animated seagull, with the gliding model as the backup.
3. Raccoon: the Quaternius raccoon.
4. Crab: built in code, with legs that scuttle.
5. Volunteers: Quaternius "Man in Long Sleeves" for both, in different shirt colors, with arms posed in code and a mallet.
6. Sand: Poly Haven "Aerial Beach 01", tinted warmer.
7. Credits: a "Thanks to" sign on the beach and a list in `team_data/project/credits.md`.
