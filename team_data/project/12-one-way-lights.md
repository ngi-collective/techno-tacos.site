# One-way guiding lights

Team request, session of 2026-11-03: "Put back one-way lights in the tunnel so the turtles can continually see light in the correct direction."

Built as: lamps along the tunnel ceiling and at the ocean end. Each lamp's lit face points back toward the box, and a dark hood covers its ocean side. A hatchling sees lamps ahead of it, and the lamps it has passed show only their hoods.

Later request: "make the tunnel lights focus in a more narrow line, so as turtles get closer the lights effectively disappear and they focus on the next light, guiding them down the path."

Built as: each lamp shines a narrow beam along the tunnel toward the box. A hatchling far from a lamp is inside the beam and sees it. Close to the lamp, it is outside the beam, so the lamp goes dark and the next one ahead takes over.
