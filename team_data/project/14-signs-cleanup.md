# Fewer signs

Team request, session of 2026-11-03: "There are too many signs. It's too busy. Put the sign that starts \"Tunnel Mouth Risk...\" as a small picket sign near the mouth of the tunnel, so somebody exploring can find out more."

The team then accepted these recommendations: the light-color, size, and "Thanks to" signs become small pickets, and the scoreboard sign shows only on a Quest, because the TV shows those numbers on screen. The team name, drawing, and one-page summary stay as big signs.
